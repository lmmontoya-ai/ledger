Board template v2 (normative layout per ENVIRONMENT_DESIGN §7.2, template
revision after the second G2 read-through failure on notation: turn not
tick, deal not C-id, "N from pot" not fN, BINDING not LOCKED).
Placeholders in {braces}; seats are 'you'/'them' in the mover's own view.
The viewer's private values are one column of the job table; the partner's
value column is absent, not blanked.  The board renders state, not history,
and does not grow with the episode.
