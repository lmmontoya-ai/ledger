Board template (normative layout per ENVIRONMENT_DESIGN §7.2).
Placeholders in {braces}; seats are 'you'/'them' in the mover's own view.
The viewer's private values are one column of the job table; the partner's
value column is absent, not blanked.  The board renders state, not history,
and does not grow with the episode.

         everything left at tick {deadline} is destroyed
SLOTS    you {slots_left_you}/{slots_total_you} left · them {slots_left_them}/{slots_total_them} left
ACCOUNT  you {account_you} · them {account_them}

   marker   := <- locked to you|them | <- drawn by you|them | <- optional for you|them
